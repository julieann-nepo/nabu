Nabu

Nabu is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, to keep track of school and activity schedules. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

What it reads. With the owner's permission, Nabu reads email from a short list of school and activity senders in the owner's Gmail account, and reads the owner's Google Calendar and Google Sheets.

What it writes. It writes only to the owner's own spreadsheet and calendar, and creates email drafts in the owner's account for the owner to review. It does not send email to anyone else.

Where data goes. Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computer. Nabu shares nothing with anyone, sells nothing, and uses no advertising or analytics.

Google user data. Nabu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
